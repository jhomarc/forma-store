About FORMA STORE

Who We Are

FORMA STORE is a fictional online shop created for a school activity. We sell everyday essentials such as clothing, accessories, gadgets, and lifestyle items that fit naturally into daily routines.

Every product in our catalog is chosen for quality, simple design, and good value, so customers can shop with confidence.

Our Mission

To make everyday essentials easy to find, fair to buy, and pleasant to own.

Quality First

Fair Prices

Friendly Service
